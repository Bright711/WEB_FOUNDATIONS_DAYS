A lightweight, floating, distraction-aware note-taking extension for Chrome.
It floats above any webpage, auto-saves notes to local storage, supports Markdown, and allows you to create, pin, and split-view multiple notes.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the app and type your note into the text field, optionally giving it a title.
  2. Tap save to add it to your list (notes get a date stamp automatically).
  3. From the list you can tap a note to update it, use the search box to find notes, or delete ones you don't need
  4. Tap the settings gear icon for in-app guidance on using the app.
